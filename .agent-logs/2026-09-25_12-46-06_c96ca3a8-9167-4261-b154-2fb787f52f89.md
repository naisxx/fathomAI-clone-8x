Three things it proves that no amount of marketing-page reading would:

**1. Action items are timestamped deep links.** `recording_playback_url` example is literally `https://fathom.video/calls/xyz123?timestamp=645` — seconds-valued. Plus `user_generated` and `completed` booleans, so items are checkable and mix AI-extracted with manual.

`Highlight` uses float `start_time`/`end_time` in seconds, while `TranscriptItem.timestamp` and `ActionItem.recording_timestamp` are `HH:MM:SS` strings — two different time representations in one API.
